M3, M4 & M5 Macs on OLX with 24 GB or more

Every olx.ro listing for an Apple silicon M3-, M4- or M5-generation Mac mini, Mac Studio or MacBook with at least 24 GB of unified memory. MacBooks and Mac Studios are carried from 36 GB up, since below that they stop being interesting here; minis are carried at 24 GB, which is as high as the model goes. Each listing was re-opened on OLX to confirm it is still for sale; the ones that have gone are struck through rather than deleted, so the price history stays readable.
